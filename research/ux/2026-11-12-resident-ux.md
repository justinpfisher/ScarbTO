### Accessibility scope

Contrast values came from live computed foreground colours and ancestor backgrounds using the WCAG luminance formula. The sampled non-large text falls below [WCAG's 4.5:1 threshold](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html). Sampled 29-pixel map controls must not be falsely labelled AA failures merely for being below 44 pixels: [WCAG 2.2 target size](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) generally uses 24 pixels with exceptions, including spacing.

Preserve tested strengths: outcome text/letters/shapes, labelled controls, pressed/expanded states, skip links, a polite result-count region, matching list access, collapsed mobile key and the area sheet's focus/Escape/return-focus behaviour. These findings are not a site-wide conformance certificate.

specimen is a **closed filled** north-up geographic silhouette with an irregular
coast, coloured neighbourhood regions, internal cream lines and a different
Scarb.TO wordmark. Shared Scarborough geography is a genuine similarity, but
the supplied specimen did not present an obvious near-copy. Different colours
or industry alone are not adequate grounds to dismiss official-mark concerns.

Preserve the closed silhouette/mosaic/wordmark distinction. Do not turn the
reduced icon into an SHN-like open angular frame or imply hospital/City
affiliation. Compare final monochrome and tiny versions, where interior detail
disappears. Escalate a material unresolved conflict before publication.
